CDS-DIR-7e65-001 · REVISION 1.0In force

Consistency of individual case records

Maintain clear separation between observed circumstances, information supplied by participants, practical observations and administrative interpretation. Preserve traceability when records are amended.

CDS-DIR-7e65-002 · REVISION 1.0In force

Use of approved terminology

Use the definitions and distinctions established in CDS-DOC-002 across administrative records, notices and procedural documentation.

CDS-DIR-7e65-003 · REVISION 1.0In force

Confidential handling of candidate circumstances

Limit case documentation to information relevant to the assessment. Avoid unnecessary disclosure of personal circumstances in general administrative communications.

Relevant characteristics and suitability of the environment.
