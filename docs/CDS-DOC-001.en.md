The English designation, Cat Distribution System (CDS), is the controlling reference for international documentation and terminology.

The abbreviations listed above are used in their respective language contexts. CDS remains the universal international identifier.

## 2. Official Description

The Cat Distribution System (CDS) is an independent system that determines the patterns governing the appearance of cats in people's lives and coordinates their allocation processes, taking into account individual circumstances and suitable conditions.

## 3. Nature of the System

The Cat Distribution System (the System) is not an institution, government department, or organisation. It does not require public confirmation of its existence.

Its presence is revealed through events, circumstances, and outcomes that may initially appear to be random.

The principles governing the System's operation are neither explained nor disclosed. Only its observable manifestations and outcomes may be documented.

## 4. Fundamental Principles

### 4.1. Comprehensiveness

The System's scope encompasses all circumstances associated with the appearance of cats in people's lives. No event is too insignificant to fall outside its attention.

### 4.2. Omniscience

The System possesses information about circumstances that may be unknown even to the individuals directly involved. An apparent absence of information does not imply an absence of observation.

### 4.3. Inevitability

An individual may be unaware of the System, refuse to acknowledge it, or have no suspicion of its activity. None of these circumstances exempts that individual from participation in allocation processes.

### 4.4. Incomprehensibility

The structure, origin, and internal mechanisms of the System are not subject to public disclosure. Its activity may be observed through its outcomes, but its underlying principles need not be understood.

### 4.5. Technological Component

CATIS (Cat Allocation & Tracking Information System) is the technological platform of the System. Its purpose, capabilities, and operating principles are accessible to System Administrators.

## 5. Relationship Between CDS and CATIS

CDS and CATIS are distinct entities.

CDS is the Cat Distribution System itself.

CATIS is the technological platform supporting the System's processes.

CATIS is not an alternative name for CDS, nor does it define or explain the nature of the System.

## 6. Statement on Randomness

**Randomness is not excluded. Its distribution is under the authority of the System.**

This statement reflects the System's relationship with events that may appear accidental or unpredictable. It does not constitute an explanation of its internal mechanisms.
