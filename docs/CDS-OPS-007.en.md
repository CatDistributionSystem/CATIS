# CDS-OPS-007

## Individual Allocation Case Handling

**Document ID:** CDS-OPS-007  
**Revision:** 7e42.12  
**Status:** Approved  
**Master language:** English  
**Document series:** Operational Procedures  
**Responsible authority:** Cat Distribution System (CDS)

---

### 1. Purpose

This procedure establishes the principles and administrative steps governing the handling of individual cat allocation cases within the Cat Distribution System (CDS).

Its purpose is to provide a consistent framework for recording, reviewing and following individual circumstances in which the appearance of a cat in a person's life may constitute an allocation case.

The procedure addresses the administrative handling of such cases without attempting to explain or disclose the underlying principles or mechanisms of the System.

### 2. Scope

This procedure applies to individual allocation cases involving:

- A cat appearing in a person's life under circumstances potentially associated with the System.
- A person identified as a potential recipient of a cat.
- An existing cat-person relationship requiring administrative recognition or review.
- An allocation case requiring further observation, clarification or documentation.

This procedure does not replace:

- CDS-OPS-001 — Response to a Cat in Distress.
- CDS-OPS-002 — Response to a Human in Distress.
- CDS-OPS-003 — Emergency Escalation Procedure.
- CDS-OPS-004 — Monitoring and Follow-up of Assistance Outcomes.

Where circumstances require immediate assistance, the applicable distress or emergency procedure takes precedence over ordinary allocation case handling.

### 3. General Principles

#### 3.1 Individual Circumstances

Each allocation case shall be considered in the context of its individual circumstances.

The existence of similarities between cases does not establish that their circumstances, requirements or outcomes are identical.

#### 3.2 Observed Circumstances

Allocation case records shall reflect observable circumstances and available information.

The System's underlying processes are not required to be established or explained as a condition of recording a case.

#### 3.3 No Presumption of Eligibility

The identification of a person as a potential recipient does not, by itself, establish that an allocation is appropriate or complete.

Likewise, the absence of a formal record does not establish that no allocation has occurred.

#### 3.4 Welfare Considerations

The well-being of the cat and the suitability of the circumstances in which it may be placed shall remain primary considerations.

Administrative convenience shall not take precedence over the welfare of the cat or the legitimate circumstances of the people involved.

### 4. Case Identification and Registration

An individual allocation case may be identified through direct observation, information supplied by an individual, reports from relevant persons or other circumstances brought to the attention of the System.

Where sufficient information is available, a case record should be established.

The record may include:

- A unique case reference.
- The circumstances in which the case was identified.
- Relevant information concerning the cat.
- Relevant information concerning the potential recipient.
- The location and circumstances of the observed interaction, where known.
- The source and reliability of the information.
- Any immediate concerns requiring separate attention.

Information not available at the time of registration should remain unrecorded rather than be replaced with assumptions.

### 5. Case Review

Following registration, the available information should be reviewed to establish the current status of the case and whether further attention is appropriate.

The review may consider:

- The circumstances surrounding the appearance of the cat.
- The nature and continuity of the interaction between the cat and the potential recipient.
- The suitability of the potential living environment.
- The welfare and immediate needs of the cat.
- Any relevant circumstances affecting the person concerned.
- Whether additional information or observation is required.

The review should remain proportionate to the circumstances and should not introduce requirements unsupported by the available information.

### 6. Case Status

For consistent administrative handling, the following status categories may be used:

**6.1 Identified**

The circumstances have been brought to the attention of the System, but the available information has not yet been reviewed.

**6.2 Under Review**

The case is being examined and relevant information is being considered.

**6.3 Pending**

Further information, observation or clarification is required before the case can be appropriately classified.

**6.4 Active**

The allocation circumstances are ongoing and require continued attention or coordination.

**6.5 Completed**

The available information indicates that the allocation circumstances have reached an appropriate conclusion and no further allocation-related administrative action is presently required.

**6.6 Inactive**

No further activity is currently recorded, but the available information does not establish that the case has been completed.

**6.7 Reopened**

A previously completed or inactive case has received new information or requires renewed attention.

A case status shall reflect its current administrative position and may be revised as circumstances change.

### 7. Case Coordination

Where an allocation case requires coordination, relevant information and activities should be recorded in a manner that supports continuity of handling.

Coordination may involve:

- Obtaining additional information.
- Communicating with relevant individuals.
- Recording significant developments.
- Identifying circumstances requiring further attention.
- Referring distress or emergency situations to the applicable operational procedures.
- Arranging follow-up where appropriate.

The recording of a case does not itself establish ownership, responsibility or authority over the cat or the people involved.

### 8. Case Outcomes

An allocation case may result in different outcomes depending on its individual circumstances.

These may include:

- An established and continuing cat-person relationship.
- A completed allocation requiring no further administrative action.
- An ongoing situation requiring further observation.
- A case in which the original circumstances have changed.
- A case in which no allocation can presently be established.
- A case remaining unresolved or unconfirmed.

The recorded outcome should reflect the available information and distinguish confirmed circumstances from reported or inferred information.

No particular outcome should be treated as mandatory merely because a case has been registered.

### 9. Documentation and Record Maintenance

Relevant case information should be maintained in accordance with applicable CATIS documentation conventions.

The record should preserve the distinction between:

- Initial observations.
- Information supplied by individuals.
- Subsequent developments.
- Administrative assessments.
- Actions taken.
- Recorded outcomes.

Changes to case status should be reflected in the record where appropriate.

The absence of additional information should not be interpreted as evidence that the circumstances have remained unchanged or that the case has been successfully concluded.

### 10. Closure and Reopening

A case may be administratively completed when the available information indicates that its allocation-related circumstances have reached an appropriate conclusion and no further administrative action is presently required.

Completion does not imply permanent certainty concerning the future circumstances of the cat or the person involved.

A completed or inactive case may be reopened where:

- New relevant information becomes available.
- Previously recorded circumstances materially change.
- Further attention is required.
- An additional allocation-related matter arises from the original case.

### 11. Relationship with Other Operational Procedures

This procedure forms part of the CDS operational framework and shall be applied in conjunction with other applicable procedures.

Where a case involves distress, emergency circumstances or the need to verify the outcome of assistance, the corresponding operational procedure shall apply.

Allocation case handling shall not delay or prevent appropriate real-world assistance.

### 12. Final Provision

Individual allocation cases shall be handled according to their circumstances, available information and relevant welfare considerations.

Registration does not predetermine an outcome. Review does not guarantee allocation. Administrative completion does not establish permanence.

**The purpose of case handling is to maintain an accurate record of the circumstances and their development, not to impose an outcome upon them.**
