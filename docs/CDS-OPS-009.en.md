# CDS-OPS-009

## Preparation and Issuance of System Communications

**Document status:** Approved  
**Document series:** Operational Procedures  
**Revision:** 7e42.12  
**Responsible authority:** Cat Distribution System (CDS)

### 1. Purpose

This procedure establishes the principles and administrative requirements governing the preparation, review, registration and issuance of official communications of the Cat Distribution System (CDS).

Its purpose is to ensure that System communications are consistent in form, appropriate to their intended purpose and properly recorded.

### 2. Scope

This procedure applies to official System communications, including:

- System notices.
- Administrative directives.
- Operational instructions.
- Formal requests and notifications.
- Administrative correspondence.
- Other communications issued in the name of the System.

It applies to communications intended for internal administrative use as well as communications addressed to external individuals or entities.

This procedure does not establish the substantive authority of any particular communication or disclose the internal mechanisms through which the System operates.

### 3. General Principles

#### 3.1 Accuracy

Communications shall accurately represent the information, decisions or instructions they are intended to convey.

Unconfirmed information shall not be presented as established fact.

#### 3.2 Clarity

Communications should use clear and unambiguous language appropriate to their intended recipients and purpose.

#### 3.3 Consistency

Official communications should follow established CDS terminology, document conventions and applicable language requirements.

#### 3.4 Appropriate Disclosure

The content of a communication shall be limited to information appropriate for its intended recipients.

Restricted information shall not be included in public or externally distributed communications unless its disclosure has been expressly authorised.

#### 3.5 Administrative Record

Issued communications shall be recorded in a manner that allows their identification and subsequent reference.

### 4. Types of Communication

For administrative purposes, System communications may be classified as follows.

**4.1 System Notices**

Communications intended to announce relevant information, changes in administrative circumstances or matters requiring awareness.

**4.2 Administrative Directives**

Formal communications establishing instructions or requirements for specified administrative purposes.

**4.3 Operational Instructions**

Communications providing guidance concerning the execution or coordination of operational activities.

**4.4 Requests and Notifications**

Communications requesting information, confirming circumstances or notifying recipients of relevant developments.

**4.5 Administrative Correspondence**

Other formal communications necessary for the continuity of System-related administrative activity.

The classification of a communication should reflect its actual purpose and should not imply authority beyond its stated scope.

### 5. Preparation

A communication should be prepared with regard to:

- Its intended purpose.
- Its intended recipients.
- The information to be conveyed.
- The action or response, if any, expected from recipients.
- Any applicable disclosure restrictions.
- Relevant supporting documentation.
- The appropriate communication category.

Where a communication refers to an existing case, directive or document, the relevant reference should be included where appropriate.

Draft communications should be distinguishable from issued communications.

### 6. Review and Approval

Prior to issuance, a communication should be reviewed for:

- Accuracy of information.
- Consistency of terminology.
- Clarity and completeness.
- Appropriate classification.
- Correct identification of intended recipients.
- Compliance with applicable disclosure restrictions.
- Consistency with relevant System documentation.

Where approval is required, the communication shall not be treated as officially issued until that approval has been granted.

The applicable approval requirements shall depend on the nature and intended use of the communication.

### 7. Registration and Identification

Official communications shall receive an appropriate administrative reference where required.

The registration record should include, where applicable:

- A unique communication reference.
- The communication category.
- Its title or subject.
- Its issue status.
- Its intended recipients or distribution scope.
- Relevant document references.
- Applicable language editions.
- Any acknowledgement or response requirements.

The registration of a communication shall not, by itself, constitute its issuance.

### 8. Issuance and Distribution

An approved communication may be issued through an appropriate distribution method consistent with its intended purpose and recipients.

Distribution may include:

- Direct communication to designated recipients.
- Inclusion in an administrative communication register.
- Publication through an authorised information channel.
- Circulation through other appropriate means.

The distribution method should take account of the communication's classification and disclosure requirements.

Where acknowledgement is requested, the requirement should be stated clearly.

The absence of acknowledgement shall not automatically establish that a communication was not received.

### 9. Language and Editions

System communications shall follow the applicable CDS language conventions.

Where multiple language editions are issued, they should convey equivalent substantive information.

The English edition shall serve as the controlling version where a discrepancy between language editions arises, unless otherwise expressly specified.

Translations shall preserve the intended meaning, classification and administrative effect of the original communication.

### 10. Amendments, Withdrawal and Supersession

An issued communication may be amended, withdrawn or superseded where circumstances require.

Any such action should be recorded and, where appropriate, communicated to the original recipients.

The record should preserve the relationship between the original communication and its subsequent versions or replacements.

An amended communication should not obscure the existence or previous status of the original.

### 11. Record Maintenance

Records of issued communications shall be maintained in accordance with applicable CATIS documentation conventions.

The administrative record should preserve, where applicable:

- The final issued version.
- Its registration reference.
- Its approval status.
- Its issue and distribution information.
- Applicable language editions.
- Subsequent amendments or withdrawals.
- Relevant acknowledgements and responses.

Drafts and working materials may be retained where they are relevant to the administrative history of the communication.

### 12. Public and Restricted Communications

Communications intended for public distribution shall contain only information authorised for public disclosure.

Restricted communications shall be handled in accordance with their applicable access and disclosure requirements.

The existence of a restricted communication may be recorded in an appropriate register without disclosing its title or contents where such information is itself restricted.

Public acknowledgement of a communication shall not imply public access to its underlying content.

### 13. Administrative Limitations

This procedure governs the administrative handling of System communications.

It does not independently establish the substantive authority of directives, determine the obligations of external parties or authorise disclosure of restricted information.

A communication shall not be interpreted as proof of circumstances beyond those expressly established in its content.

### 14. Final Provision

A communication shall be prepared according to its purpose, issued according to its classification and preserved according to its administrative significance.

The preparation of a communication does not constitute its approval. Registration does not constitute issuance. Issuance does not establish acknowledgement.

**An official communication is part of the administrative record of the System, not a disclosure of the System itself.**
