# CDS-REG-001

## System Notices and Administrative Communications

**Document ID:** CDS-REG-001  
**Revision:** 7e65.4  
**Status:** Approved  
**Master language:** English  
**Subject:** Classification, identification, acknowledgement, and recordkeeping of System Notices

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the terminology and general recordkeeping conventions for notices issued within the administrative context of the Cat Distribution System (CDS).

It provides a consistent framework for identifying, classifying, acknowledging, and retaining System Notices. It does not describe the internal mechanisms of CDS or establish how the System initiates, determines, or carries out any process.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to administrative records designated as System Notices and to references to those notices in CATIS.

A System Notice is a recorded communication intended to convey information, an administrative direction, or a matter requiring attention by its designated recipients.

The existence of a notice does not, by itself, explain the circumstances that gave rise to it or disclose the underlying processes of CDS.

## 3. Notice Classification

Each notice is assigned one primary classification:

- **Informational** — records information for awareness or reference; no response is necessarily required.
- **Operational** — communicates a matter relevant to the continuity or coordination of established administrative activity.
- **Administrative** — records an administrative instruction, clarification, or change in documented practice.

Classification describes the purpose of a notice. It does not indicate the importance, urgency, or expected outcome of the matter.

## 4. Priority

Where appropriate, a notice may also be assigned a priority:

- **Routine** — for ordinary circulation and reference.
- **Elevated** — requires particular attention from its designated recipients.
- **Priority** — requires timely attention in accordance with the notice itself.

Priority is independent of classification. A notice may be informational, operational, or administrative at any priority level.

## 5. Identification

System Notices are assigned unique identifiers using the following format:

`CDS-NOT-[CSR]-[SEQUENCE]`

For example: `CDS-NOT-7e65-007`.

The identifier consists of:

1. the `CDS-NOT` designation;
2. the applicable CATIS System Reference (CSR) period;
3. a sequence number distinguishing the notice within that period.

Identifiers are permanent references. A notice identifier must not be reassigned to another notice.

## 6. Notice Status

The status of a notice describes its place in the record:

- **Issued** — formally entered into the notice record.
- **Active** — remains applicable for reference or attention.
- **Superseded** — replaced by a later notice or administrative record.
- **Archived** — retained for historical reference and no longer maintained as an active notice.

A status change does not erase the notice or alter its identifier. Where a notice is superseded, the related record should be cross-referenced where appropriate.

## 7. Acknowledgement

Acknowledgement is recorded separately from notice status. Where acknowledgement is required, the following terms may be used:

- **Pending** — acknowledgement has not yet been recorded.
- **Acknowledged** — receipt has been confirmed by the designated recipient or recipients.
- **Recorded** — the acknowledgement has been entered into the administrative record.

Acknowledgement confirms receipt only. It does not necessarily signify agreement, approval, or completion of any action described in the notice.

Where acknowledgement is not required, the record may indicate **Not required**.

## 8. Distribution and Recipients

A notice should identify its intended recipient group or administrative scope where such information is appropriate to the record.

Distribution details must be limited to what is necessary for the notice's administrative purpose. Publicly accessible records must not expose personal information or details that could compromise the privacy of individuals involved.

## 9. Recordkeeping

The notice record should preserve, as applicable:

- the unique notice identifier;
- the notice classification and priority;
- the notice status;
- the CSR reference;
- the subject and concise description;
- the intended recipient group;
- acknowledgement status, where applicable;
- references to related or superseding records.

Corrections and status changes should be reflected in the record without obscuring the original notice identity.

## 10. Confidentiality and Disclosure

System Notices are administrative records. Their existence or publication does not authorise disclosure of confidential information, personal details, or the undisclosed internal mechanisms of CDS.

A public summary, where issued, is a separate publication and must be prepared for that purpose. It must not be treated as a substitute for the underlying administrative record.

## 11. Relationship with CATIS

CATIS may be used to present, reference, and maintain System Notice records.

This document defines recordkeeping conventions only. It does not establish particular software functions, interfaces, notification channels, access controls, or technical capabilities of CATIS.

## 12. Final Provisions

This document forms part of the CDS documentation framework. It shall be interpreted consistently with the foundational description of CDS, the terminology framework, and the applicable CATIS documentation.

Nothing in this document shall be interpreted as an explanation of the nature or internal operation of the Cat Distribution System.
