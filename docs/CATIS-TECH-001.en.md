# CATIS-TECH-001

## CATIS Technical Platform Overview

**Document ID:** CATIS-TECH-001  
**Revision:** 7e65.4  
**Status:** Approved  
**Master language:** English  
**Subject:** Cat Allocation & Tracking Information System (CATIS)

---

## 1. Purpose

CATIS (Cat Allocation & Tracking Information System) is the technological platform supporting the processes of the Cat Distribution System (CDS).

This document establishes the platform's position within the CDS framework and defines the scope of its technical documentation.

It does not constitute a technical specification or disclose internal mechanisms of the System.

## 2. Position Within the CDS Framework

CDS and CATIS are distinct entities.

- **CDS** is the Cat Distribution System itself.
- **CATIS** is the technological platform supporting the System's processes.

CATIS is not an alternative name for CDS, nor does it define or explain the nature of the System.

The existence of a technological platform does not alter the fundamental nature of CDS.

## 3. Administrative Access

The purpose, capabilities, and operating principles of CATIS are accessible to System Administrators.

This access is part of the established relationship between the System and its technological platform.

The present document does not define administrative roles, permissions, authentication procedures, or access-control mechanisms.

## 4. Documentation Scope

Technical documentation relating to CATIS may be developed as the platform is further defined.

Such documentation may address, where formally established:

- platform specifications;
- interfaces;
- protocols;
- operational procedures;
- supporting technical materials.

No capability, component, interface, or protocol shall be considered an established feature of CATIS solely because it appears in a proposal or preliminary document.

## 5. Documentation Principles

CATIS documentation shall follow these principles:

1. **Terminological consistency.** CDS and CATIS must remain clearly distinguished in all documentation.
2. **Factual accuracy.** Confirmed characteristics must be separated from proposals and assumptions.
3. **Controlled specification.** Technical capabilities and requirements must not be presented as established until formally defined.
4. **Canon consistency.** Documentation must not contradict the fundamental principles of CDS.
5. **Limited disclosure.** CATIS documentation must not be used to explain or disclose the undisclosed internal mechanisms of CDS.

## 6. Status of Technical Specifications

This document does not establish any particular software architecture, implementation technology, data model, interface, protocol, or operational capability.

Such matters remain outside its scope until separately defined and documented.

Future technical documents may supplement this overview without changing the distinction between CDS and CATIS.
